About HARUMA Academy

Empowering Minds,

Located in the heart of Hawassa, Ethiopia, HARUMA Academy is a premier educational institution dedicated to bridging the global skills gap. We offer international-standard training in languages, cutting-edge technology, multimedia, and artificial intelligence.

Our Mission

To empower students, graduates, and professionals with practical, globally-demanded skills for unlimited career opportunities.

Our Vision

To be the leading hub of innovation, language mastery, and digital transformation in East Africa.
