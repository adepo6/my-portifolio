Who We Are

A Legacy of Compassion in the Heart of Hawassa.

Founded over a decade ago, Majet Charity Association is a registered non-governmental organization operating primarily in Hawassa and surrounding regions. We believe that every individual deserves dignity, opportunity, and a supportive community.

Our Mission

To provide essential support, education, and resources to vulnerable children and families, empowering them to break the cycle of poverty.

Our Vision

A society where every child has access to basic needs, quality education, and the opportunity to realize their full potential.

Our Focus Areas

How We Make a Difference

Our comprehensive approach addresses the multifaceted challenges faced by vulnerable communities.

Educational Sponsorship

We provide school supplies, uniforms, and cover tuition fees for orphaned and vulnerable children, ensuring they stay in school.

Learn more

Health & Nutrition

Combating malnutrition through our daily feeding programs and providing basic healthcare access to families in extreme poverty.

Learn more

Women's Empowerment

Providing vocational training and micro-loans to single mothers, enabling them to start small businesses and become self-reliant.

Challenges We Face

Understanding the landscape of our work.

Economic Instability

Rising living costs heavily impact the vulnerable families we support, increasing the demand for basic provisions.

Resource Limitations

Consistent funding is required to maintain long-term programs like educational sponsorships and daily feeding.

Infrastructural Gaps

Reaching remote areas around Hawassa remains a logistical challenge for our field volunteer teams.
